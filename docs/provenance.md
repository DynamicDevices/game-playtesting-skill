# Provenance and scope

Original instructions are adapted from Dynamic Devices' MIT-licensed
[Nail Reveal skill](https://github.com/DynamicDevices/nail-reveal-ai-skill/tree/4830f7855cfedd641d48efce600435fecaa5cdc2).
The MIT copyright notice, Copyright (c) 2026 Dynamic Devices, is preserved in
[LICENSE](../LICENSE). Alex requested a separate public repository for Jon,
Claude guidance, general game/app testing and a simpler linked Nail Reveal skill.

Reusable lessons include controlled contact tests, screenshot-grounded states,
finite batches/persistent input, crop mappings, supported fallbacks, restoration,
reproducible findings and honest separation of progress counts from quality.
Game-specific timings, coordinates and tool results remain in the original repo.
Recordings/game assets are linked, not copied or relicensed.

Alex also supplied feedback that Jon wants to find possible disengagement points,
compare solutions and explore imaginative features. This package turns that into
a method for observations, hypotheses, prototypes and experiment proposals.
It does not claim that Jon commissioned app changes, consented players or
authorised analytics, and does not reproduce private chat screenshots or unrelated
conversation details. No message has been sent to Jon.

Newly written debug-build examples and synthetic session records are proposals,
not evidence of existing app hooks or successful tests. There is no production
instrumentation, engagement model or live A/B-testing system here.
